## 4. Project scope

- **Accepted scope:** Logo & Typography package. Brand workshop (shorter session), primary logo (two concepts, one revision round, colour exploration), primary and secondary typography, file exports, basic playbook (logo and typography usage).
- **Fee / hours:** £2,600 / 26 hours.
- **Deferred to later phases:** full colour system, tone of voice and verbal identity, comprehensive guidelines, sub-brand guidance (Prefera / Prefera Business), UI starter kit, launch assets.
- **Out of scope:** web design, web development, videography.
- **Key dates:** handover target end of October 2026; launch December 2026 (possibly earlier).

## 5. What we know

- **Products / sub-brands / personas:** (1) Prefera app for individuals, digitally active 18 to 40 in Saudi Arabia. (2) Prefera Business web platform for organizations with large customer bases. One brand across both. The brand is for the product, not the company.
- **Competitors named by the client:** Didomi, Usercentrics, PossibleNOW.
- **Brands they admire, and why:** Apple (simplicity, intuitive design, privacy), Spotify (effortless personalization), Airbnb (warm, human, trust).
- **Three words:** Respectful. Intelligent. Empowering.
- **Existing lines:** "From unwanted marketing to wanted interactions." / "Communicate with Preference." / "Verify preferences. Communicate with confidence." / "Reach the right person, through the right channel, at the right time."
- **Visual constraints:** Simple, minimal, no animation-led identity. Must scale from app icon to large format and expand into future phases. Arabic-first bilingual typography. App vendor hard-codes fonts, so licensing and developer handover matter. Current app uses coral, business platform uses blue, both with a "P" in a rounded square.
- **Known gaps to close in the session:** commercial objectives and launch targets; pricing tiers / minimum volume; Arabic spelling of the name; Prefera vs Prevera; whether the Digital Persona becomes a named product.
