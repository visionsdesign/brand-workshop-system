> The workshop now runs as a web app. This file is the reference that links each registry slide to the Figma frame it was rebuilt from (`src/lib/registry.ts` carries the same `figmaNode`). ## Differences from the DESIGN.md registry

1. **No D04 Wrap-Up divider.** S25 Summary has the gradient background and works as the close. Either add D04 to the master or remove it from the registry.
2. **Two slides missing from the registry:** the Contents / Workshop Agenda slide (A01) and the Break slide (X01). I propose adding both as registry rows.
3. **The objective line is on the Contents slide, not the Title.** Registry S00 says "Title / Workshop Objective". On the master, `{{WORKSHOP_OBJECTIVE}}` belongs in `131:214`.
4. **Stale client copy:** `131:214` still reads "…the strategic DNA of SHOUT transitioning your brand from a traditional agency into a scalable, investable creator infrastructure platform." It needs to become a token.
5. **Agenda labels don't match the dividers.** The Contents slide says "The Engine (40mins) / The Access Points / Visual & Verbal / The Summary", but the dividers say "The Core DNA / Alignment & Audience / Narrative & Voice". The timing is also hard-coded.
6. **The Break slide's header says "The Core DNA"** even though it sits in the Alignment & Audience row.
7. **S12 card copy uses American spelling:** "signs the check". British is "cheque". Copy change for Dom/Dan to decide.
8. **S22 has two "pending" placeholders** (`131:680`, `131:681`). These are answer areas to fill live or at Capture, not tokens.
